My Professional Journey

A story of growth, creativity, and a commitment to building meaningful digital experiences

2022

Where It All Began

2022 was the year everything opened up for me. What started as curiosity quickly grew into a passion for creating digital experiences. I spent countless hours learning web design, graphics, and digital marketing, experimenting with ideas and building my foundation one project at a time. This phase shaped my mindset, discipline, and love for the craft.

Learning Design Digital Skills
2023

Taking the First Step

In 2023, everything became real. I started building websites and designs for actual clients, and Ephraim Creations began forming its identity. Each project taught me the value of communication, structure, and understanding what clients truly need. This was my first introduction to impacting small businesses through design and technology.

Freelance Client Work Portfolio Building
2024

Growth, Exposure & Momentum

2024 was the year I stepped into visibility. I expanded my network, collaborated with other creatives, and shared my journey online. My brand gained trust, my skills matured, and I started working with more established businesses. This season was about sharpening my voice, building confidence, and understanding that strategy matters just as much as design.

Brand Growth Collaboration Community
2025

Building a Brand That Creates Impact

Today, I am focused on scaling Ephraim Creations into a strategic digital partner for small businesses and startups. This chapter is about building systems, refining my services, and creating value through web design, development, and SEO. It is also a season of mentoring upcoming creatives and helping others grow through the academy and the content I share.
